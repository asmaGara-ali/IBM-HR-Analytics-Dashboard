# 👥 IBM HR Analytics & Workforce Attrition Dashboard

## 🎯 Business Overview & Problem Statement
Understanding workforce dynamics and employee turnover is vital for HR strategy. This interactive multi-page Power BI dashboard explores employee demographics, compensation structures, work-life balance factors, and promotion timelines across **1,000 employees** and **3 departments** to uncover key attrition drivers.



## 🔑 Key Metrics & Insights Across Pages
* **Workforce Profile:** 1,000 Total Employees across 3 Departments (Research & Development leading with 961 staff).
* **Overall Attrition Rate:** **16.12%** (237 employees left).
* **Overtime Impact:** Overtime work significantly correlates with higher employee turnover rates.
* **Age & Generation:** Attrition peaks heavily in the **26–35 age group**.
* **Compensation Trends:** Average Monthly Salary is **$6.50K** with an average tenure of **7 years**.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Business Intelligence:** Power BI (Multi-page dashboard with custom navigation buttons)
* **Key Features:** Page Navigation (Overview, Attrition Drivers, Compensation & Growth), Scatter Plots, Area Charts, Stacked Bar Charts

## 💡 Key Actionable Recommendations
1. **Overtime Management:** Review workload distribution to reduce overtime strain, a primary driver of employee exit.
2. **Mid-Career Engagement:** Focus career development programs on employees in the 26–35 age bracket.
3. **Promotion Timelines:** Optimize promotion pathways for employees experiencing long promotion lag times.
